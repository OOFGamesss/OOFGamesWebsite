Spin the reel, crack the coffer, and see what the vault gives up.

About the plugin

A loot box opener for your venue. You build a prize pool across five rarity tiers, set the odds for each, and generate a private link for one player. They open it in a browser, and a horizontal reel of vault coffers scrolls past a marker, slows, and lands on one. The coffer's colour is its rarity, and it opens to reveal which prize from that tier they won. A win guide listing everything the vault can pay out stays on screen the whole time, so nobody is spinning blind. The plugin tells you what to trade the moment a spin lands, and tracks how much of each prize is left.

Key Features

Five Rarity Tiers

Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic and Legendary, in grey, green, blue, purple and gold. Rename any of them per session to suit your venue.

Odds You Control

Set the weight of every tier yourself. The reel is built to match, so a one percent Legendary really is one tile in a hundred going past.

Prize Stock Tracking

Give each prize a quantity. Offer one mount and you can only ever owe one. A sold-out prize leaves the draw and greys out on the win guide automatically.

Always-On Win Guide

Players see every prize, its tier, its image and how many are left, plus the live chance of each tier, before and during every spin.

Up To Five Reels At Once

Buy a stack of spins and open up to five at a time. The reels stop one second apart, so each result lands on its own beat.

Provably Fair

The server decides every spin from a secret and publishes a fingerprint of it before the reel moves. The secret is released with the result, so any player can verify their own spin afterwards.

Nothing To Read Ahead

The page is never sent the result until the reel is already at full speed, and the server refuses to answer any earlier. Opening devtools shows a player nothing but a reel.

Trade Queue

Every win appears in the plugin within seconds, with the player, the tier and the prize, and a button that targets them and opens a trade.

Prize Catalogue and Custom Art

Pick prize images from the shipped catalogue or paste your own image link. Everything is preloaded before the player can spin, so a reveal never waits on a download.

Reusable Vault Presets

Save a prize pool and its odds as a named preset, then export it as a code to share with other hosts at your venue.

Local History

Every vault you run and every prize you have paid out is kept on your own machine, not on a server, and stays there after the session closes.

One Link Per Player

A link is private to one player and carries the spins you granted. Top it up mid-session if they buy more, or end it whenever you like.

How a session runs

  1. 1. Build your prize pool in the Vault section: prizes, images, stock counts, tier names and odds.
  2. 2. Take payment in game, then generate a link for that player with however many spins they bought.
  3. 3. Send them the link with one click. They open it and spin.
  4. 4. Wins appear in your Payouts queue. Hit Trade, hand the prize over, mark it paid.
  5. 5. When the spins run out the page stays open a few minutes so they can look at what they won, then closes. Another go means a fresh link.

Installation

  1. 1. Type /xlsettings in the in-game chat to open the Dalamud settings window.
  2. 2. Navigate to the Experimental tab.
  3. 3. Paste the following link into the Custom Plugin Repositories field at the bottom:
https://puni.sh/api/repository/oof-games
  1. 4. Click the + button, ensure the repository is set to Enabled, then click Save and Close.
  2. 5. Type /xlplugins, search for Ishgardian Vaults, and click Install.
  3. 6. Get a game key from the OOF Games Discord with /keys in #game_keys, and paste it into the Sessions section.
