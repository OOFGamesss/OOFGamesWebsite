Bardic Memory

Guess the tune from FFXIV's orchestrion library.

About the plugin

Bardic Memory is a Dalamud plugin that turns the game's own orchestrion library into a music guessing game. Each round picks a random track and plays you the opening of it, starting with just half a second. Name it from that sliver for six points, or stretch the clip through 1, 2, 4, 8, and 15 seconds, dropping a point each time you do. Wrong guesses cost nothing, so you can keep naming songs at the length you are on. When the round ends, a reveal names the track and where it plays, lets you hear the full piece, and adds the result to a history of every round you have played.

Key Features

The Real Opening of the Track

Every clip is the true start of a piece from the game's orchestrion library, read straight from the game files and played through the game's own music system.

Six Points, Then Fewer

Half a second is worth six points, then five at 1 second, four at 2, three at 4, two at 8, and one at 15. Give up and the round scores nothing.

Wrong Guesses Are Free

Guess as often as you like at whatever clip length you are on. Only stretching the clip costs you points.

Pick Your Category

Play the whole library or narrow the pool to one orchestrion category, from locales and dungeons through to raids, trials, and seasonal music.

Search as You Type

Every named roll is searchable, ranked by title first, then description, then the places and duties the track plays in, with a hint under each result.

Titles in Any Language

Search for a track by its English, Japanese, German, or French title, whichever one you know it by.

A Proper Reveal

The end of every round names the track, with the roll's own description and the zones and duties it plays in, then lets you hear the full piece or go on to the next song.

Clip Volume

A volume slider beside the play button runs from 0 to 100 per cent, so the clip sits at the level you want.

Only the Clip Is Heard

The game's background music is held silent while a round is running and handed back the moment it ends. You can switch this off in the settings.

Skip Solved Songs

Keep songs you have already named out of the pool until every song in the category has been solved.

Full Round History

Every finished round is logged with running totals for rounds, solves, points, average score, and streak.

Four Languages

The plugin window is translated into English, Japanese, German, and French.

Interface

The window opens with /bardicmemory and holds four tabs: Play, History, Settings, and Support.

Play

A strip of category buttons sits above six rows, one for each points value, and each row is marked Skipped as you stretch past it. Below them, a bar shows how far the clip runs, with a play button and volume control underneath. Type in the search box and pick a song, and the button beside it changes from Increase Duration to Submit. Once the clip is at its full 15 seconds, it offers Give up instead.

Play: The Reveal

A panel over the play tab shows your score and the track's name, along with the roll's description and where it plays. From there you can choose Play full track, Next song, or End Game.

History

Five tiles across the top show rounds played, solve rate, total points, average score, and your current and best streak. Below them, a table lists each round's song, score, and time, newest first. Times are shown as ST, which is server time.

Settings

Silence the game's music during a round is on by default, and Skip songs already guessed correctly is off. Rounds kept in history defaults to 500 and can be set anywhere from 10 to 5,000. Clear history and stats wipes every recorded round and total, and asks you to confirm first.

Support

A short FAQ covering how to play, where the songs come from, why your music goes quiet during a round, and what a wrong guess costs. A button at the bottom opens the OOF Games Discord.

Commands

  • /bardicmemory Open or close the window.

Clips cannot be heard while an orchestrion is playing where you are, so switch it off before you start. If you use the Orchestrion plugin, turn off its song replacement while you play.

How to install

Bardic Memory is on the official Dalamud plugin list, so there is no custom repository to add.

  1. 1. Type /xlplugins in the in-game chat to open the plugin installer.
  2. 2. Search for Bardic Memory.
  3. 3. Click Install.
