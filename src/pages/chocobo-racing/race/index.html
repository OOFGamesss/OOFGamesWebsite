How to Play

  1. 1
    Trade Gil to your host

    Hand over some in-game Gil to your race host so you're ready to bet.

  2. 2
    Get your PIN

    Your host will give you a 6-digit PIN. Tap "Enter PIN to bet" at the top of the page and enter it to log in.

  3. 3
    Pick your chocobos

    Head to the Runners section and tap each chocobo you fancy backing.

  4. 4
    Set your stakes

    Open your Bet Slip and type in how much Gil you want to bet on each chocobo you picked.

  5. 5
    Place your bet

    Hit "Place Bet", then sit back and wait for the race to begin!
