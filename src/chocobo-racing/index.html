About the plugin

Bring racing to your own venue with this host suite for Classic betting races and Raffle races. Designed for hosts who want a professional, automated event, the plugin covers everything from the first bet or registration through to the final payout. In Classic mode, players back a chocobo and the host advances the field with in-game /dice. In Raffle mode, each player becomes a numbered chocobo, the server draws the winner, and the race plays out live on the website. Spectators can watch either mode online, and Classic players can place bets on the site with a PIN.

Key Features

Classic and Raffle Modes

Run a traditional betting race, or switch to raffle racing where registered runners become numbered chocobos and the website draws the winner.

Automatic Bet Detection

In Classic, the plugin reads party and alliance chat in real time and logs bets the moment a player types them, so no manual bookkeeping is needed from the host.

Integrated Bank System

Each player's balance is tracked automatically, with add, take, and tip controls, a one-click trade button, auto payout, private tells, and cash-out alerts when a player asks in chat.

Live Webview and PIN Betting

Mirror the session to the website so anyone can watch live. Classic players can bet online with a PIN from the Banks tab. Raffle races must be live on the webview, because the draw runs on the site.

Console and Vanilla Client Friendly

All race commentary and results are posted directly to in-game chat so console players and unmodded clients can follow every moment without extra software.

Scalable Race Size

Classic races run from two chocobos up to ten, configurable before each race from Classic Race settings.

Adjustable Track Length

Set the Classic finish line between 5 and 15 yalms, or stretch a raffle track from 5 to 100 yalms for a longer show.

Decimal Payout Odds

Set a single payout multiplier for the field to control your house edge, with automatic payout calculation at the end of each Classic race.

Perfect Race

Optionally pay higher odds when only the winning chocobo ever advanced: every roll was their number and no other bird moved.

Raffle Prizes and Registration

Offer a gil pot with host boost and venue cut, an item, or a custom prize. Runners join from nearby players, a chat keyword, or a right-click menu, with paid entries numbered onto the grid.

Customisable Chat Profiles

Edit every automated message, including Advertise shouts, Classic race calls, and raffle registration or winner lines. Named presets can be saved, exported, and imported between hosts.

Full Race History and Reporting

A complete log of every Classic and Raffle round is stored for review, alongside a profit and loss tracker so hosts can monitor margins over time.

Interface

The plugin is organised through a sidebar. Race expands to Classic and Raffle. Settings expands to Classic Race, Classic Chat, Raffle Chat, and Sounds. Support expands to Help and Game Key.

Race: Classic

The control panel for taking and recording bets. You must be in a party or alliance to use Classic host controls. Race Info lets you announce rules, shout your Advertise message, and repost the chocobo list while betting is open. Race Control opens betting, calls last bets, closes betting, starts the race, rolls /dice, voids a race, or ends the round. Once the race starts, the tab shows a live racetrack. When it concludes, winners, winnings, and updated bank balances are listed. Payouts are processed from the Banks tab, not automatically.

Race: Raffle

A raffle race needs a live web session first: open Webview, enter your game key and venue details, then press Go Live. On setup you choose a gil pot (host boost plus an optional venue cut), an item, or a custom prize, set the entry fee (zero is free), pick a finish line from 5 to 100 yalms, and optionally enable a chat join keyword and a closing time in Server Time. Open Registration, add runners from nearby players, the join keyword, or a right-click Add to Raffle menu, then mark fees paid. Paid runners receive a grid number, and you can still adjust the pot boost while registration is open. Close once at least two eligible runners are in, start the race, and the website draws the winner and plays it out. Afterwards you can announce, trade, or auto-pay a pot prize. Advertise is available in every raffle phase and shouts the message from Raffle Chat settings.

Banks

Classic only: this tab is disabled during raffle races. A table of party members plus any external players you add. Collect funds into each player's bank, add or remove gil, or keep an amount as a tip. Tell buttons send a private balance message, Tell All Balances walks the whole list, Trade opens a trade window, and Auto Payout handles bulk payouts. Add people from the nearby-player list, or right-click a player (or their name in chat) and choose Add to Chocobo Racing. External players can bet, but they will not see party or alliance rolls in chat. When a player types cash out in chat, their row is highlighted. While a web session is live, each bank also has a PIN you can generate and tell so they can bet on the website.

Webview

Start and stop the live website session. Enter your game key, set the venue name and image shown on the site, then Go Live. Copy the spectator link to share with guests. Classic players need their PIN from the Banks tab to bet online. Raffle hosts must be live here before registration can open. Presets can also be selected, exported, and imported from this tab.

Settings

Four sections, plus named presets on Classic Race, Classic Chat, Raffle Chat, and Webview. Presets cannot be switched or edited while a race or raffle is running. Classic Race covers chocobo count (2-10), finish line (5-15 yalms), max bet per chocobo, payout odds for the field, optional Perfect Race odds, and chocobo renaming. Classic Chat edits every automated Classic message, including Advertise, betting calls, winner text, bank-balance tells, web PIN tells, and emotes, plus whether the bet list is split by chocobo or by player. Raffle Chat covers raffle Advertise, registration, closing time, winner, fee-request, and runner-invite messages, with toggles to auto-announce registration, auto-announce the winner, and auto-tell a paid runner their race link. Sounds can alert you when a runner joins the raffle by typing the join keyword.

History

A complete log of every round, tagged as Classic or Raffle, showing the winner, the bets or payouts, and tools to delete a round or a whole session. Useful for reviewing individual events, settling disputes, or understanding betting patterns across your events.

Profit/Loss

A running summary of your net position across all sessions, breaking down total bets taken, total payouts made, tips, and your overall margin so you can track performance over time and calculate any percentage fees owed to your venue owner.

Support

Help covers common questions for bug reports, feature requests, and software commissions, with links to the OOF Games Discord and website. Game Key explains how to claim a free key from the #game_keys channel with /keys, what it unlocks for the live webview, and the rules for keeping it. Keys must never be shared.

Commands

  • /chocoboracing Open the main plugin window.
  • /cr Alias for /chocoboracing.
  • /chocoboracingconfig Open directly to the settings tab.

How to install

  1. 1. Type /xlsettings in the in-game chat to open the Dalamud settings window.
  2. 2. Navigate to the Experimental tab.
  3. 3. Paste the following link into the Custom Plugin Repositories field at the bottom:
https://puni.sh/api/repository/oof-games
  1. 4. Click the + button, ensure the repository is set to Enabled, then click Save and Close.
  2. 5. Type /xlplugins, search for Chocobo Racing, and click Install.
