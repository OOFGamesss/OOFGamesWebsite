How to play

  1. 1. Invite the player to your party so everyone can see the dice rolls, then select them from the party list.
  2. 2. Collect the entry cost from the player before their turn begins.
  3. 3. The player rolls /dice for their starting number. That opening roll earns a coin too, and sets the ceiling for the next one.
  4. 4. The player rolls /dice again using their last result as the new maximum. Every result other than a 1 earns a coin and becomes the next ceiling.
  5. 5. Use the 'Ask to Roll' button at any point to tell the player exactly which dice command to type next.
  6. 6. Rolling a 1 ends the turn and banks their coin count on the leaderboard. The most coins wins the pot - shared between all winners when multiple winners are enabled.

Key Features

Validated Roll Chain

Only the player taking their turn is scored, and every roll's maximum must match their previous result, so a stray dice roll from anyone else in the party cannot corrupt a turn.

Configurable Opening Roll

Leave the starting roll max at 999 for a plain /dice opener, or set it lower to start every player from a tighter ceiling and shorten the average run.

Optional Instant Win

Set a target coin count that wins the game outright the moment a player reaches it, or leave it off and let every player roll until they bust.

Boosted Pot and Trades to Pot

Add a venue-side boost to the prize pool and choose what percentage of trade revenue feeds the pot, holding the remainder back for the venue.

Live Leaderboard

Every player's best coin count is tracked in real time, highlighting the biggest hoard reached this session and who is currently winning.

Winner Payout

When the session finishes, the winner screen shows each winner's share and lets you announce, trade, or auto-pay the payout, with a progress bar tracking what has been paid.

Custom Chat

Manual Trigger Messages

Rules Sent from the 'Send Rules' button on the Game panel to post the how-to-play summary and the current pot.
Advertise Sent from the 'Advertise' button on the Game panel to shout the game to the zone and pull in new players.
Request Gil Sent from the 'Request Gil' button on the Game panel to prompt the current player to pay their entry.
Request Gil (Buyer) Sent from the 'Request Gil (Buyer)' button when someone else is paying, telling the buyer the cost and who they are paying for.
Announce Pot Sent from the 'Announce Pot' button on the Stats panel to broadcast the current pot total.
Win Shout Sent from the 'Announce Winner' button on the session winner screen, including the winner's share of the pot.

Auto Messages

Ask to Roll Sent from the 'Ask to Roll' button with the exact dice command the player needs next, and their running coin count once they have collected at least one. Also fires automatically after each roll if auto-send is enabled.
Announce Score Sent from the 'Announce Score' button when a turn ends and the player is not in the lead. Also fires automatically if auto-send is enabled.
Announce Lead Sent from the 'Announce Lead' button when a turn ends and the player has taken the lead. Also fires automatically if auto-send is enabled.

Stats panel
