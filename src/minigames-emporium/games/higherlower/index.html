How to play

  1. 1. Invite the player to your party so they can see the dice rolls, then select them from the party list.
  2. 2. Collect the entry cost from the player before their turn begins.
  3. 3. Roll /dice (the configured dice size) to set the opening number.
  4. 4. The player calls Higher or Lower for the next roll - by typing it in party chat or by the host clicking the button.
  5. 5. Roll again. A correct call extends their streak; a wrong call ends the turn. Rolling the same number is a no-op, so roll once more.
  6. 6. Each player's best streak is recorded on the leaderboard. The most rounds wins the pot - shared between all winners when multiple winners are enabled.

Key Features

Guess Detection from Chat

Players call higher or lower right in party chat - the plugin reads h/high/higher and l/low/lower automatically - or the host clicks the Higher and Lower buttons directly.

Configurable Dice and Auto Win

Set the dice size the host rolls and, optionally, a target round count that wins the game instantly the moment a player reaches it.

Boosted Pot and Trades to Pot

Add a venue-side boost to the prize pool and choose what percentage of trade revenue feeds the pot, holding the remainder back for the venue.

Live Leaderboard

Every player's best streak is tracked in real time, highlighting the highest round reached this session and who is currently winning.

Single or Multiple Winners

Let everyone tied on the top streak share the pot, or crown a single outright winner - your choice per session.

Winner Payout

When the session finishes, the winner screen shows each winner's share and lets you announce, trade, or auto-pay the payout, with a progress bar tracking what has been paid.

Custom Chat

Manual Trigger Messages

Rules Sent from the 'Send Rules' button on the Game panel to post the how-to-play summary and the current pot.
Request Gil Sent from the 'Request Gil' button on the Game panel to prompt the current player to pay their entry.
Announce Pot Sent from the 'Announce Pot' button on the Stats panel to broadcast the current pot total.
Win Shout Sent from the 'Announce Winner' button on the session winner screen, including the winner's share of the pot.

Auto Messages

Let's Play Sent from the 'Let's Play' button after payment is verified. Also fires automatically once payment clears if auto-send is enabled.
Ask Guess Sent from the 'Ask Guess' button to prompt the player for their call. Also fires automatically after each roll while awaiting the guess if auto-send is enabled.
Announce Score Sent from the 'Announce Score' button when a turn ends and the player is not in the lead. Also fires automatically if auto-send is enabled.
Announce Lead Sent from the 'Announce Lead' button when a turn ends and the player has taken the lead. Also fires automatically if auto-send is enabled.

Stats panel

The Stats panel at the bottom of the Game tab tracks the live pot and session figures.

Total Pot Boosted pot plus the configured share of trade revenue, split between all winners.
Boosted Pot Gil put in by the venue to boost the prize pool.
Taken in Trades Total Gil received from players during the current session.
Kept from Trades Portion of trade revenue held back for the venue when Trades to Pot is below 100%.
Players Played Number of players who have taken a turn so far this session.
Highest Rounds The best streak reached by any player this session.
Currently Winning The player, or players, currently on top of the leaderboard.
