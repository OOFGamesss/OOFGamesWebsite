How to play

  1. 1. Set the ticket cost, boosted pot, max tickets per player, and an optional closing time, then start the session.
  2. 2. Add players manually, from the nearby list, or let them join automatically by typing the keyword in chat.
  3. 3. Collect Gil by trade - tickets are granted automatically based on the amount received, and any leftover under one ticket's cost is kept as credit toward their next trade.
  4. 4. Grant or remove tickets manually at any time - this is how free raffles, with the ticket cost set to zero, hand out numbers.
  5. 5. When ready, arm the draw and roll /random up to the highest ticket number sold.
  6. 6. Whoever holds the rolled number takes the whole pot. If it lands on a gap left by a removed player, clear the draw and roll again.

Key Features

Automatic Ticket Allocation

A completed trade works out how many tickets it covers and grants the next free numbers on the spot, with no manual entry required.

Ticket Credit Carryover

Gil left over under the cost of one ticket is held as credit against the player's name and combines with their next trade toward a whole ticket.

Gap-Preserving Numbering

Removing a player frees their numbers as a permanent gap. Everyone else keeps their exact numbers - nobody is ever renumbered.

Optional Closing Time

Set a close time in Server Time to show a live countdown and drive a closing announcement. Ticket sales stay open until the host draws.

Boosted Pot and Trades to Pot

Add a venue-side boost to the prize pool and choose what percentage of ticket revenue feeds the pot, holding the remainder back for the venue.

Winner Payout

Once a winner is drawn, announce, trade, or auto-pay the pot straight from the winner screen, with a progress bar tracking what has been paid.

Custom Chat

Manual Trigger Messages

Request Gil Sent from the 'Request' button next to an unpaid player to prompt them to trade in the ticket cost.
Request Gil (Buyer) Sent from the 'Request Gil (Buyer)' button in the buyer popup to a third party paying for a player's tickets.
Announce Pot Sent from the 'Announce Pot' button on the Stats panel to broadcast the current pot total.
Tickets Sold Sent from the 'Tickets Sold' button in the Shouts panel to broadcast how many tickets have sold so far.
Closing Time Sent from the 'Closing Time' button in the Shouts panel to broadcast the close time and how long is left.
Join Reminder Sent from the 'Join Reminder' button in the Shouts panel to invite players to enter with the join keyword. Keyword join mode only.

Auto Messages

Ticket Numbers Sent from the 'Send Numbers' button beside a player holding tickets. Also fires automatically when a trade grants them tickets, if auto-send is enabled.
Announce Winner Sent from the 'Announce Winner' button on the draw result screen, including the winning number and the pot. Also fires automatically the moment a winner is drawn, if auto-send is enabled.

Stats panel

The Stats panel at the bottom of the Game tab tracks the live pot and ticket figures.

Total Pot Boosted pot plus the configured share of ticket revenue.
Boosted Pot Gil put in by the venue to boost the prize pool.
Kept from Trades Portion of ticket revenue held back for the venue when Trades to Pot is below 100%.
Ticket Cost Gil per ticket, or "Free" when the cost is set to zero.
Players Number of players who currently hold at least one ticket.
Tickets Sold Total tickets sold so far this session.
