Deathroll Tournament

Bracket-based elimination. One player walks away the champion.

Screenshots coming soon

How to play

  1. 1. Collect the entry cost from each player before the tournament begins.
  2. 2. Add all entrants, optionally shuffle, then configure best-of settings per round.
  3. 3. Both players in each match roll /random 10 - the highest roller goes first.
  4. 4. The first player does /random. The result becomes the cap for the opponent.
  5. 5. Play alternates back and forth. The first to roll a 1 loses that game.
  6. 6. Winner advances through the bracket. Final winner takes the entire pot.

Key Features

Automatic Bracket Generation

Once paid players are seeded, the plugin builds a single-elimination bracket instantly with bye slots filled automatically to reach the nearest power of two.

Configurable Best-of Series

Set a different best-of count per round before the tournament starts, so early rounds play out fast while semi-finals and finals get the time they deserve.

Registration and Payment Tracking

Players enter via a configured join keyword and the host marks each one as paid. Only confirmed paid players are seeded into the bracket.

Live Bracket Display

The Bracket tab shows the full draw in real time with the current match highlighted and completed results displayed as players advance through the rounds.

Discord Webhook

Connect a Discord webhook and the plugin posts and live-updates a single embed throughout the event, progressing automatically from registration through to the final result.

Eight Automated Chat Templates

Fully customisable messages for bracket announcements, matchups, first roller determination, rerolls, match wins, round wins, tournament winner, and pot announcements.

Custom Chat

Manual Trigger Messages

Request Gil Sent from the 'Request Gil' button per player in the registration table to prompt them to pay their entry.
Request Gil (Buyer) Sent from the 'Request Gil (Buyer)' button to a third party paying the entry fee on behalf of a registered player.
Announce Bracket Sent from the 'Announce Bracket' button in the game panel. Posts the round announcement alongside the bracket matchups.
Announce Pot Sent from the 'Announce Pot' button on the Stats panel to broadcast the current pot total.

Auto Messages

Announce Matchup Sent from the 'Announce Matchup' button in the match tracker. Also fires automatically when a new match begins if auto-send is enabled.
Announce Winner Sent from the 'Announce Winner' button on the tournament complete screen. Also fires automatically when the tournament winner is decided if auto-send is enabled.
Re-roll Random 10 Sent from the 'Re-roll Random 10' button in the match tracker when both players tie. Also fires automatically on a tie if auto-send is enabled.
First Player Sent from the 'First Player' button in the match tracker after order rolls resolve. Also fires automatically when the first player is determined if auto-send is enabled.
Announce Round Win Sent from the 'Announce Round Win' button in the match tracker when a game ends but the series continues. Also fires automatically if auto-send is enabled.
Announce Match Win Sent from the 'Announce Match Win' button in the match tracker when the series is decided. Also fires automatically when a player is eliminated if auto-send is enabled.

Stats panel

The Stats panel tracks live tournament figures from the moment registration opens.

Total Pot Combined entry fees and boosted pot available to the tournament winner.
Boosted Pot Gil put in by the venue to boost the prize pool.
Entry Cost Gil owed by each player to enter the bracket.
Players Entered Total number of paid players seeded into the tournament.
Current Round The active round number within the bracket.
