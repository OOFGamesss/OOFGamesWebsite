      How to Play

      1. 1
        Pick your numbers

        Choose 4 main numbers from 1 to 15 plus 1 bonus ball from 1 to 5 - or hit Lucky Dip and let fate decide.

      2. 2
        Buy your tickets

        Each ticket costs 100,000 gil from your OOF account balance. No ticket limit! You can also buy through any OOF Games host in game.

      3. 3
        Watch the draw

        Every week the numbers are drawn live in game with genuine /random rolls, then replayed right here in the ball machine. Sales close 5 minutes before the draw.

      4. 4
        Get paid instantly

        Prizes land straight in your OOF account the moment the draw settles. Match all 4 plus the bonus to take the jackpot - if nobody does, the pool rolls over and keeps growing!

        The figure at the top of the page is the jackpot pool, not a guaranteed payout. The four fixed prizes are paid out of that pool first, and the jackpot winner receives whatever is left, shared equally if more than one ticket wins it. A week with a lot of fixed-prize winners leaves less behind for the jackpot.
